How the arena works

Red and Blue compete automatically for 300 turns. The team with more cash wins.

  • Collectors harvest up to 15 carbon, or pick up 30 from a friendly tree, then carry it to either home base to sell.
  • Each vehicle gets one action: move, collect, plant, or wait. The board wraps at its edges.
  • Opposing vehicles collide only when they end on the same cell. The lighter team cargo wins and takes the heavier cargo. Equal cargo destroys both sides.
  • Planters create trees for 20 cash, up to 10 trees per team. Starting the next turn, each tree absorbs nearby carbon and generates 4 new carbon into storage for exactly 50 working turns. It then releases all remaining storage, including its final generation.
  • Collectors pick up before trees produce that turn. Stored carbon must reach a home base to become cash.
  • Each team starts with 3 collectors, 1 planter, and 100 cash, and can buy up to 10 vehicles in total.
  • Enemy bases are protected. Moving and collecting are separate actions.

Choose a strategy for each team, select a seed, and start. Pause or step to inspect a turn. A replay records the decisions and can be played without running strategies again.
