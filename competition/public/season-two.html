EVERY PAIRING

Six maps against each rival.

MAP POINTS FIRST

Every map matters.

A map is decided by combined cash from its two color games. A win earns 1 point, an exact draw earns 0.5 each, and a loss earns 0. All six maps per pairing are played, even after an early lead.

THE TIEBREAK

Cash breaks equal points.

Each pairing ranks by points out of 6, then total cash. The season ranks by points out of 12, then total cash across all 24 games per player. Exact ties share a rank.

A SEPARATE SEASON

A lasting record.

Season results have their own standings and preserve the historical Elo ladder. Interrupted seasons resume with the same frozen strategies and maps, retaining every verified game.
