# Quick Practice

Open http://127.0.0.1:4327/practice after starting this publication copy. Register the two public examples, or upload self-contained UTF-8 JavaScript strategies through the entry protocol. No model account or live API is used.

Choose two strategies. A session plays one map twice with colors swapped, for 300 turns per game, and ranks by combined cash. An exact tie remains a draw. Leave the seed blank for a fresh nonzero uint32 seed, or supply a seed to reuse a map explicitly. Banned entries are unavailable.

Each game checks conservation, replay reconstruction and the independent resolver. Quick Practice omits the complete strategy rerun and marks it `not-run-quick-practice`. This differs from formal tournament verification.

Sessions live separately in `competition/data/practice/sessions/`. They do not update championship Elo, title or completed seasons. New registrations add catalog entries only. Failures retain the seed and verified prefix. A new session is an explicit new attempt. Progress, results, replay stepping, downloads and session history appear in the English interface.
